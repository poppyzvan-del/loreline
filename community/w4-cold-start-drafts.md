## 草稿 1 · Reddit r/gamedev（价值长帖）

**标题**：I localized ARPG dialogue for years—here are 5 rules that stop English players from "feeling the translation"

**正文**：

Been doing bilingual ARPG dialogue (CN↔EN) for a few years on AAA and mobile titles. The thing that breaks immersion for English players is almost never a wrong word—it's syntax and tone. A line that reads clean in Chinese turns into a manual when you shove it into one English sentence.

Five rules I keep coming back to, with before/after:

**1. Short sentences. Split one into two if you have to.**
Players in combat read a line in half a second. Long relative clauses = no line at all.
- ❌ "Don't drag it out for too long, we should resolve this quickly and cleanly."
- ✅ "Don't drag it out. Quick and clean."
My personal cap: 8 words max per combat line.

**2. Active voice. Less passive.**
Chinese uses passive sparingly. Mirror that into English and the protagonist sounds like a bystander.
- ❌ "This opponent is not easy to be dealt with."
- ✅ "This one's on a whole different level."

**3. Don't translate mood words—swap in an equivalent *sentence shape*.**
Words like 果然 / 原来 / 倒是 have no word-for-word match. Don't hunt for one.
- ❌ "As expected, it is this kind of work again."
- ✅ "Here we go again. Same kind of errand."
The fix isn't a word. It's a phrase English speakers actually say.

**4. Transcreate culture-specific terms. No pinyin.**
"Jianghu" means nothing to an English player and costs them 2 seconds they don't have.
- ❌ "In this Jianghu, reputation is life."
- ✅ "In this line of work, your name is all you've got."

**5. Read it out loud. If it doesn't sound like something a person would say, rewrite it.**
This catches more than any CAT tool. Protagonists don't say "Although it is painful, as long as I do not fall, it is acceptable." They say "It hurts. But hurt isn't down."

**The one-line summary:** you're not translating the character—you're *re-performing* them in English.

## 草稿 2 · Indie 游戏开发 Discord（短帖/频道消息）

> Discord 不发长帖，发短消息 + 答问。这条适合 #localization 或 #showcase 频道（先看频道规则，很多 #showcase 限周发一次）。

**消息**：

Quick thing I keep seeing in indie ARPG/mobile localization: the English version reads fine word-by-word but players say it "feels translated." Nine times out of ten it's one of these—

• Long relative clauses where Chinese packed a lot into one sentence
• Passive voice making the protagonist sound like a narrator
• Mood words like 果然/原来 getting a forced word-for-word match instead of a native phrase swap

Fix is usually structural, not lexical. Shorten. Active it. Swap the *sentence shape*, not the word. Read it out loud—if it doesn't sound human, rewrite.

## 通用冷启动原则（W4 执行清单）

1. **养号 2-3 天再发**：在 r/gamedev、r/IndieDev、目标 Discord 先评论别人帖、答疑问，建立存在感。
2. **give first，永远**：所有帖以价值内容为主，链接只在 profile / DM / 文末一句软引。
3. **5 个免费送换 review**：主动找 5 个在做 ARPG/动作向的独立开发者，私信送旗舰 Pack，请他们用后在 itch.io / Discord 给一句真实反馈。这 5 条 review 是后期转化的种子。
4. **盯评论区 24h**：发帖后认真回每条评论，这是社区信任建立的核心。
5. **Product Hunt 发布**：W4 末或第二周初发，准备一句 tagline + 创始人 comment 讲 why。
