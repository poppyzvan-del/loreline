## 4. 创始人开场 Comment（发布后第一时间自己发，讲 why）

> PH 文化：创始人发布后立刻在评论区发一条"why I built this"。真诚 > 营销。以下为草稿，按你的真实语气微调。

```
Hey PH 👋 — I'm the one behind Loreline.

I spent 4+ years as a narrative designer on AAA ARPG and mobile titles, doing bilingual CN↔EN dialogue. The same two problems kept showing up on every project:

1. Combat and system dialogue always lost the budget fight. Localization covered the main story; everything else got placeholder text or rushed machine translation — and English players could smell it.
2. Freelance translators drifted. One shipped "抽卡" as gacha, the next as draw, the next as summon. Three months in, nobody could agree on a baseline tone.

Loreline is my attempt to package the boring, repeatable part of that work so indies can skip it. The first pack is 100 bilingual ARPG combat lines — each tagged by scene and emotion, so you can wire them into a state machine and trigger by game state. The English is written to native-speaker standard: short, active, no translation smell. The second is a 50-term localization glossary that pins drifting terms to one recommended form.

It's not a SaaS. It's not a course. It's the kind of asset pack I wish I'd had on day one of a project — drop it in, calibrate your team against it, move on to the work that actually needs a human.

Free 8-line sample on the site. Happy to answer anything about bilingual game writing in the comments. 🎮
```

**中文核对要点**（自己看，不发布）
- 讲清楚"为什么"：4 年叙事设计经验，每个项目都遇到两个问题——战斗台词总输给预算、外包译员调性漂移。
- 产品定位：不是 SaaS、不是课程，是"我希望项目第一天就有的资产包"。
- 结尾给价值：免费 8 条样例 + 评论区答疑（建立信任，不硬推）。

## 7. 应对话术（评论区高频问题预案）

| 可能被问 | 简短答法 |
|---|---|
| "Why $29 for 100 lines?" | 100 条 × 母语级双语润色，按外包单价折算每条不到 $0.3；省的是你自己润色的几小时 + 调性不统一的返工。 |
| "Can I use these in a commercial game?" | 可以，商用授权，可用于项目内对话系统。仅禁止把 Pack 本身转售或二次分发。 |
| "Will this fit my specific protagonist?" | 调性偏坚毅冷静。如果你的男主是另一型，先拿免费 8 条样例试；后续会出女性向/其他语气版本。 |
| "Why not just use ChatGPT to generate lines?" | 可以，但你会花更多时间在统一调性、修机翻味、给外包译员定基准上。这个 Pack 是"基准调性"本身。 |
| "Chinese only?" | 双语对照交付，CN + EN。英文已达母语级，可直接落进英文版项目。 |
